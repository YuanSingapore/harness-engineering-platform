# Scoring Strategy: English Buddy

## Purpose

The scoring system is designed to encourage active participation and provide immediate positive feedback after each question.

## Rules

- **Correct answer:** +5 points
- **Incorrect answer:** +0 points (no penalty — mistakes are learning opportunities)

## Score Display

- The current score must always be visible during a session
- Display the score prominently in the **top-right corner** of the interface
- Update the score **immediately** after each question

## Future Enhancements

- Daily high score
- Weekly achievements
- Badges
- Streak bonuses
